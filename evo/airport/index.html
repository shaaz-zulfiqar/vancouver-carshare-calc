The $5 drop-off fee

Ending a trip at either YVR location adds a $5.00 drop-off fee automatically.

BCAA members don’t pay it.

Templeton Station

  • Cars sit in marked Evo stalls outside the station’s North exit.
  • Drive in from Grant McConachie Way and follow the signs into the Evo Zone lot.
  • Park in a marked Evo stall and end the trip in the app.

Park’N Fly

  • Enter at the main Miller Road entrance.
  • Leave the car with the valet attendant and note the licence plate.
  • Go inside to register the car, then end the trip in the app.
  • To pick up a car, show your Evo app at the checkout counter.

Source: YVR’s Evo page and evo.ca/Rates. YVR’s page doesn’t say whether separate airport parking charges apply to Evo cars, so check the Evo app before you leave the car.
