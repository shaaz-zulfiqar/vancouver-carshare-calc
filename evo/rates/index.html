How the caps work

You pay $0.49 for each minute until the charge reaches the hourly rate. That happens at about 37 minutes, so a trip of 37 minutes or more costs the same as an hour.

The same thing happens at the day rate. Past roughly 5½ hours, one day at $104.99 is cheaper than paying by the hour.
