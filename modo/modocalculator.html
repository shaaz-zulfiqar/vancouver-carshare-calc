Modo FAQ

When does the Day Tripper kick in?

Whenever it costs less than regular hourly plus distance charges for your plan. For example, a long day of 12+ hours often lands on the $100 day rate. It includes 500 km a day on Plus and 250 km on Monthly, and every extra kilometre is 35¢, so very long drives can still get expensive. The calculator compares both options and shows the cheaper one.

Why is distance a separate charge?

Modo bills for booked time and kilometres driven, so a short errand with a long drive can cost more than the hours suggest.

Should I pick Modo or Evo?

It depends on standing time versus distance. Modo is cheaper when much of your booking is spent parked ($5–6 an hour versus Evo's meter of up to $17.99), while Evo is cheaper when you cover a lot of kilometres, since it has no per-km charge. Compare both for your trip to see the exact distance where the winner flips.
