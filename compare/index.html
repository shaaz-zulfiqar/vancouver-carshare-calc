Quick rules of thumb

  • Standing time favours Modo. It costs $5–6 an hour, while Evo's meter keeps running at up to $17.99 an hour even when you are parked.
  • Distance favours Evo. Its rates include gas, insurance and unlimited km, while Modo adds 35¢ for every kilometre.
  • Day Tripper km run out. Modo includes 500 km a day on Plus and 250 on Monthly, then charges 35¢ per extra km, so very long drives usually flip to Evo.
  • Bookings of 3+ days favour Evo. Its 3-day ($264.99) and 5-day ($424.99) rates undercut Modo's Day Tripper, even before any kilometres.
