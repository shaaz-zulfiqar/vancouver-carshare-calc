Which is cheaper: standing time or distance?

Evo bills for time only. Modo bills for time plus distance. So the winner depends on how much of your booking is spent standing still and how far you actually drive.

Standing time favours Modo

Modo's hourly rate is $5 (Plus) or $6 (Monthly). Evo's meter runs at up to $17.99 an hour whether you are driving or parked. Appointments, shopping and long stops are where Modo saves the most.

Distance favours Evo

Evo includes gas, insurance and unlimited kilometres in its rates. Modo adds 35¢ per km, and its Day Tripper only includes 500 km (Plus) or 250 km (Monthly) per day, so long drives get expensive fast.

Evo

  • BCAA members: free registration (the one-time fee is $35) and 10% off driving rates, up to $110 a year. evo.ca/rates
  • Multi-day rates: 3 days for $264.99 ($50 off) and 5 days for $424.99 ($100 off). Built into the Evo calculator.

Modo

  • Refer a friend: members earn $25 in driving credits when a friend joins. modo.coop/get-more
  • Cleaning and Snow Angels: earn up to $35 in free drive time by cleaning a Modo after driving it, or clearing snow off one.
  • Seat sales: selected vehicles are discounted during periodic sales, applied automatically at booking. Amounts vary.
